# Getting Access

The AI Assistant is currently available to the University of Arizona community.

## Joining an Existing Workspace

If your instructor or team lead has already set up a workspace, they can add you as a member using your University of Arizona NetID. Once added, sign in at [chat.cyverse.ai](https://chat.cyverse.ai/) and your workspace will appear on the dashboard.

## Requesting a New Workspace

If you need a new workspace for your course, lab, or team, you can request access through the [workspace request form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdWX9Nf9gsJ4vJ9r1FBIrW61QE60T9tl6uEbxFjTut3mUATEg/viewform){target=_new}.

You can also find the request form by visiting [chat.cyverse.ai](https://chat.cyverse.ai/) and clicking **Get Access**.

## Requirements

- A University of Arizona NetID
- Institutional login credentials (WebAuth/SSO)
