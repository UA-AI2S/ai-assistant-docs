# About AI Assistant

The University of Arizona AI Assistant is an open source platform that facilitates access to commercial and on-premise large language models (LLMs) with budget and access controls. It is built for teams, labs, and courses across the university.

## Key Features

- **Designed for small teams and courses** — Use the AI Assistant for labs, classes, and research groups with shared access and controls.
- **Bring Your Own (BYO) LLM** — Instructors and team leads can bring their own commercial or third-party LLM, or use CyVerse's on-premise models.
- **Built for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — Instructors can optionally configure the AI Assistant to provide answers strictly from source documents.
- **Budget management and cost savings** — Control model access, set budgets at the workspace level, and set per-member spending limits.
- **Built-in guardrails** — Ensure responsible, safe, and effective use of AI within your team or course.
- **API access** — Use your API key with any OpenAI-compatible client, IDE, or CLI tool.

## How It Works

Each workspace in AI Assistant represents a team or course. Workspace admins (typically instructors or team leads) configure which LLM models are available and manage member access. Members receive an API key they can use to interact with the available models through the web interface or external tools.
